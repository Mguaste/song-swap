Song Swap

Trade songs with friends, one track at a time.

Song Swap is a free music-sharing app. Swap a song with a friend each day, rate what you got, and see how compatible your taste is. Paste a link from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, or Tidal and it opens in whatever app your friend uses, including Amazon Music.
