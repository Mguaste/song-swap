What is a song swap?

A song swap is when friends trade songs with each other, usually one track at a time, so each person gets a song picked just for them. It's a simple way to share the music you love and discover something you'd never find on your own.

Unlike sharing a giant playlist, a song swap is small and personal. One song is easy to actually listen to, and because a friend chose it for you, it comes with a story: why they love it, where they first heard it, or why it made them think of you.

How a song swap works

  1. Pick your swap partner. A close friend, a sibling, a partner, or someone whose taste is totally different from yours (those are often the best swaps).
  2. Agree on a rhythm. One song a day works well. It's enough to stay in touch without becoming homework.
  3. Send one song each. Choose something you genuinely love, not just what's popular right now.
  4. Listen with an open mind. Give it a real listen, ideally the whole song.
  5. React. Rate it, say what you liked, or send a song back that it reminded you of.

Song swap ideas and themes

Free choice is great, but a theme makes a song swap more fun and pushes you to dig deeper into your library:

  • A song from the year you were born
  • The song you'd play to introduce someone to your favorite artist
  • Something you loved in high school
  • A song in a language you don't speak
  • The best song under two minutes
  • A cover that's better than the original
  • A song for a road trip, a rainy day, or a late-night drive
  • A deep cut: no songs with more than a million plays
  • A song your parents played when you were a kid

Song swaps for groups

Song swaps aren't just for two people. Friend groups, clubs, and classrooms do them too: everyone sends a song to the person next to them, or the group picks a weekly theme and everyone shares a pick. Pairing people up in rotating swaps is a great way to get a group talking about music.

The problem: everyone uses a different music app

The biggest hassle with swapping songs is that your friend is on Apple Music and you're on Spotify, or one of you uses YouTube Music. Sending a link that opens in the wrong app means copying the title, searching for it, and hoping you find the right version.

How to do a song swap with Song Swap

Song Swap is a free app built for exactly this. You and a friend trade songs one at a time, and every song opens in whichever app each person uses.

  • Works across streaming apps. Paste a link from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, or Tidal, and your friend opens it in their own app, including Amazon Music.
  • Add friends with a code. Share a six-character friend code. No email, no phone number, no searching.
  • Rate what you got. Rate the songs your friends send and see a compatibility match for each friend based on your ratings.
  • A fresh swap every day. Current songs reset at midnight, so there's always a reason to send a new one.
  • Every swap is saved. Look back at everything you and a friend have traded, with duplicate detection so you never send the same song twice.
  • Notifications. Get notified the moment a friend sends you a song.

Frequently asked questions

Is Song Swap free?

Yes. It's free, has no ads, and never asks for your Spotify, Apple, or other streaming login.

Do my friend and I need the same music app?

No. That's the whole point: each of you picks the app you listen on, and songs open there.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Song Swap runs in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

How many songs should we swap?

One at a time works best. It keeps each song special and gives you time to actually listen.
